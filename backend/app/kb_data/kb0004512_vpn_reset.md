id: KB0004512
title: Resetting your VPN client
category: Network > VPN
last_reviewed: 2026-06-15
---

If your VPN client (GlobalProtect) is failing to connect or dropping
intermittently:

1. Sign out of GlobalProtect completely.
2. Clear the local profile cache (Settings > Advanced > Clear Cache).
3. Sign back in using your SSO credentials.
4. If the issue persists after reconnecting, restart the GlobalProtect
   client service from Task Manager > Services.
5. If you still cannot connect, check for an active network incident
   before opening a new ticket -- your issue may already be tracked.
